Get Started in 3 Simple Steps

From registration to reconnecting with classmates — it takes less than 5 minutes.

Register

Fill in your details, choose your batch year and last studied class, and create your account with a secure password.

Verify & Login

Your account is verified by the school HOI. Once approved, log in using your registered email and password.

Reconnect

Browse the alumni directory, filter by batch, and rediscover old friends. Update your profile anytime.

Your Privacy, Our Priority

We built the portal with strict privacy controls. Here's exactly what's visible and what's not.

✅ Publicly Visible

Your name, batch year, and profile photo appear in the alumni directory so classmates can find you.

✅ Visible to You Only

Your full profile — including school details, contact info, and profession — is only visible to you when you log in.

🔒 Always Private

Your email, mobile number, address, date of birth, and all other personal details are never shown to other alumni.

⚠️ HOI Access

The current HOI (Head of Institution) can review profiles for verification and account management purposes only.
